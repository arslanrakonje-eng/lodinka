Welcome to Lodinka!

Jump across the floating blocks, collect coins, and reach the green goal. Use the arrow keys or WASD. On mobile, use the buttons.

Your adventure

Try to reach the finish platform without falling. This is a small playable starter game you can expand.

Mobile controls

Hold a direction to move. Tap JUMP to hop. You can also use keyboard controls on a computer.

How to play

Move to the next block, jump over gaps, collect the gold coins, then touch the green goal block. If you fall, you restart at the beginning.

Lodinka is an independent fan-made block game and is not affiliated with Roblox.
